What we believe

Built on principles.
Not just possibilities.

A few commitments guide every design decision, every workflow, and every new capability.

PRINCIPLE / 01

Human agency first.

AI should make people more capable. Important decisions, permissions, and final approvals belong to the human using the product.

PRINCIPLE / 02

Private by intention.

Local-first design and explicit user choice guide how workspace data is handled. External AI use should remain transparent and controlled.

PRINCIPLE / 03

Progress you can verify.

Software evolution should be traceable: build, test, verify, and lock — with no silent changes to trusted baselines.
